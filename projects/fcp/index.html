Pro Tips

Execution details that turn a calendar into actual leads.

  • Best posting windows: Tue/Thu/Sat mornings (couples browse together on weekends) and Tue/Thu evenings (post-work scrolling).
  • Story strategy: Reshare every feed post to Stories with a poll or question sticker ("Are you having a videographer? 👍👎") to boost engagement signals.
  • Lead capture: Rotate DM keyword CTAs — "FILM," "GUIDE," "STYLE," "RENTAL" — each keyword tells you exactly which content drives leads.
  • Reels priority: Turn social proof and BTS posts into Reels — wedding content performs 2–3× better in video format.
