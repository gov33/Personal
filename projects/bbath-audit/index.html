Overview

Executive Summary

B.Bath has a compelling brand, clinical credibility, and earned press — but the website is a leaky bucket at every stage of the customer journey.

B.Bath is positioned as a Sensory-Safe™ skincare brand for babies, children, and expectant moms. It has EWG Verified™ status, dermatologist-reviewed formulations, and retail kiosks at Cloudnine Hospitals. Yet the store has almost zero organic traffic and zero sales.

This is not a product problem — it's a discovery + trust + conversion problem. The site is technically underperforming, SEO-invisible, conversion-hostile, and leaking trust at every step.

🎯 The Core Diagnosis

  • Zero discovery: No sitemap, no blog, no keyword optimization, no backlinks. The brand is invisible in search.
  • Broken navigation: The /collections page is empty, and there's no visible category menu.
  • No social proof: Zero customer reviews on product pages — a dealbreaker for skeptical parents.
  • No retargeting: No email capture, no abandoned cart flows — 98% of visitors leave forever.
  • UX friction: Slow load times, duplicated announcement bar, sold-out products, unclear CTAs.

📈 Expected Impact

With the 30/60/90-day action plan, B.Bath can go from zero traffic and zero sales to 500+ monthly visitors and 10–20 monthly orders within 90 days — if the fundamentals are executed properly.

Lens 1

Technical Analysis

Page speed, architecture, crawlability, scripts, and foundational health.

✅ What's Working

  • SSL/HTTPS properly implemented (Shopify standard).
  • robots.txt correctly disallows admin/cart/checkout paths.
  • Shopify platform provides reliable hosting and uptime.
  • COD (Cash on Delivery) is available and displayed — critical for Indian e-commerce trust.

🚨 What's Broken

  • No sitemap.xml — /sitemap.xml returns an error. This is a critical SEO failure.
  • Duplicate announcement bar — "COD Available Orders Dispatched in 24 hours" appears six times in a row, pushing down the fold and creating CLS.
  • Heavy third-party scripts — Judge.me, chat widget, analytics pixels add render-blocking latency.
  • /collections page is broken — shows only a chat widget, no collection navigation.
  • /blogs returns an error — no blog exists despite the brand having a rich story.
  • No internal linking — products are isolated; no cross-sells, no breadcrumbs.
  • Default Shopify search — no autocomplete, no intelligent ranking.

💥 Why It Hurts

  • Google cannot find your pages without a sitemap. This alone explains much of the "no organic traffic" problem.
  • Slow load times cause 53% of mobile users to abandon a site that takes >3 seconds.
  • The duplicated announcement bar wastes above-the-fold real estate and signals poor attention to detail.
  • No blog = no content marketing = no organic discovery.

🔧 Fixes — Prioritized

  • Critical Generate and submit sitemap.xml to GSC — Quick Win
  • Critical Remove 5 of 6 duplicate announcement bar lines — Quick Win
  • High Defer off-screen third-party scripts — Medium
  • High Compress and lazy-load product images — Medium
  • High Create /blogs and publish 5–10 SEO articles — Medium
  • Medium Audit and remove unused Shopify apps — Quick Win
Lens 2

Structure & UX / CRO

Navigation, homepage clarity, product pages, cart, checkout, and trust signals.

✅ What's Working

  • Product pages have clear "How To Use" sections with expandable content.
  • Key ingredients highlighted with visual badges (Ceramide Complex, Hyaluronic Acid, etc.).
  • Family-reported insights (e.g., "95% felt skin appeared smoother") are prominently displayed.
  • Shipping and returns policy is accessible from product pages.
  • Contact information (email + phone) is visible.

🚨 What's Broken

  • No main navigation menu — no visible category navigation; /collections is broken.
  • Can a new visitor find products in <3 clicks? No. No clear path to "all products" or ranges.
  • Value proposition is buried — the hero section is unclear; above the fold shows only duplicated announcement bar + product cards with no context.
  • No clear "Shop Now" CTA above the fold.
  • Hydrating Face Butter shows as "Sold out" — key products unavailable.
  • Variant selection is unclear — no size/volume options visible.
  • "Add to Cart" button is present but not visually emphasized.
  • Trust signals (EWG Verified, dermatologist-tested) are buried on the About page, not on product pages.
  • Cart page shows "Your cart" but no clear path to checkout.
  • Payment options are not listed before checkout — Indian customers want to see UPI, COD, cards upfront.
  • No urgency/scarcity cues — no "only X left," no countdown timers.
  • Low color contrast on some text elements; likely missing alt text on images.

🔧 Fixes — Prioritized

  • Critical Build navigation menu with "Baby Range" and "Family Range" — Quick Win
  • Critical Fix /collections page to show all collections — Quick Win
  • Critical Create hero section with clear value proposition + CTA — Medium
  • High Restock sold-out products or mark "Coming Soon" — Quick Win
  • High Add payment icons (UPI, COD, Cards) to product and cart pages — Quick Win
  • Medium Add urgency cues (low stock indicators) — Quick Win

Lens 4

SEO Analysis

Keyword targeting, titles, headers, internal linking, schema, backlinks, and indexation.

✅ What's Working

  • Product pages have unique titles and descriptions.
  • robots.txt correctly configured to avoid duplicate content issues.
  • Brand has earned press coverage — backlink opportunities exist.

🚨 What's Broken

  • No keyword research evident — products optimized for "Sensory-Safe™" (zero search volume) instead of high-volume terms like "best baby moisturizer for sensitive skin India."
  • Homepage title appears to be generic Shopify default.
  • Product page titles are product-name only, not keyword-optimized.
  • No meta descriptions — critical for click-through rates.
  • Homepage uses decorative headings (✦Chosen by Families✦) instead of semantic H1–H3 tags.
  • No clear H1 on the homepage — Google cannot understand the page.
  • Almost zero internal linking — products are isolated, no "related products," no breadcrumbs.
  • Product schema likely present but not enhanced with reviews or ratings.
  • No Review schema — reviews aren't visible.
  • No Breadcrumb schema — no breadcrumb navigation exists.
  • No Organization schema — missing or incomplete.
  • No sitemap = incomplete indexation.
  • Backlink profile is weak — competitors have thousands of backlinks from review sites and blogs.

🔧 Fixes — Prioritized

  • Critical Generate and submit sitemap.xml to GSC — Quick Win
  • Critical Optimize all title tags and meta descriptions with keywords — Quick Win
  • Critical Create SEO-optimized blog content (10+ articles) — Medium
  • High Add proper H1–H3 structure to homepage and all pages — Quick Win
  • High Implement Product, Review, Breadcrumb, and Organization schema — Medium
  • High Build internal links between related products — Quick Win
  • Medium Reach out to press contacts for backlinks — Medium

Synthesis

Root-Cause Synthesis

The top 3–5 root causes most likely responsible for low traffic + low conversions + no sales.
1 SEO Invisibility (Zero Discovery)

The site has no sitemap, no blog, no keyword optimization, and no backlinks. Parents searching for "sensory safe baby skincare India" or "best moisturizer for baby eczema" cannot find B.Bath. This is the primary reason for zero organic traffic.

2 Broken Information Architecture (Can't Find Products)

The /collections page is broken, there is no visible navigation menu, and products are not organized into logical categories. A new visitor lands on the homepage, sees no clear path to browse, and leaves. This is the primary reason for high bounce rate.

3 Missing Trust Signals on Product Pages (No Reviews)

EWG Verified™ and clinical testing credentials are buried on the About page — not displayed on product pages. There are zero customer reviews. Indian parents buying skincare for their babies are highly skeptical; the absence of reviews kills conversion.

4 No Content Marketing / Email Capture (No Retargeting)

No blog, no email popup, no welcome flow, no abandoned cart emails. When 98% of visitors leave without buying (normal e-commerce behavior), there is no way to bring them back. This is the primary reason for no sales despite some traffic.

5 Technical Performance & UX Friction

Slow load times, duplicated announcement bar, missing sitemap, sold-out products, and unclear CTAs create a poor user experience that further depresses conversion among the few visitors who arrive.
